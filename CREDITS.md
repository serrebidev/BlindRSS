# Credits

BlindRSS exists because RSS readers are mouse-first and podcast players bury the show under visual clutter. This one is fast, screen-reader-friendly, and plays audio dependably.

Third-party projects it uses:

- yt-dlp for YouTube, Rumble, and Odysee media.
- VLC and ffmpeg.
- Deno.
- Caster's casting engine for Chromecast, AirPlay, DLNA/UPnP, Sonos, Roku, and Kodi. `caster_config.py` is vendored verbatim from Caster 0.5.23 by `tools/sync_caster.py`; fix it in Caster, not here.
- Hosted providers (Miniflux, Inoreader, The Old Reader, BazQux) and feed discovery sources (Apple Podcasts, gPodder, Feedly, NewsBlur, Feedspot, Reddit, the Fediverse, and others) are services this app reads, not code from this project.
